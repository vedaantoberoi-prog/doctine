Your records stay yours.

Health records are personal. In Doctine, your documents are stored privately in your account and aren't visible to other users.

When you scan a document or ask a question, it's sent securely to the AI service that reads it for you. You decide what to keep.

Doctine supports your care. It doesn't replace your doctor.

Doctine gives general health information. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose. Always follow your doctor's advice about your treatment.
