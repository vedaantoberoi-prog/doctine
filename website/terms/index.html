2. What Doctine does

Doctine lets you store and organise your health records, keep track of medicines and doses, and keep a health profile. 3. Not medical advice

  • The information Doctine gives, including the assistant's answers and the details it reads from documents, is general information. It isn't medical advice, a diagnosis or a prescription.
  • The assistant is an AI. It can be wrong or incomplete, and it can misread documents. Check important details against the original document and with your doctor.
  • Always follow your doctor's advice. Don't start, stop or change a medicine or treatment because of something Doctine says.
  • Medicine reminders are a convenience. They may not appear if your device is off, offline or silenced, or if the app is closed. Don't rely on them alone for time-critical medicines.
